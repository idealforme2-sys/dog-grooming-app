🐾 FREQUENT QUESTIONS 🐾

Got Questions? We Have Answers! ♡

Everything you need to know about our salon care, safety rules & appointments

🫧
100% Fear-Free Atelier
Serene, cage-free comfort
🐾

"Every pup is treated with patience, gentle handling, and undivided 1-on-1 attention."

— Elena, Certified Master Stylist

To guarantee the safety and health of all furry guests in our atelier, we require up-to-date Rabies, DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza), and Bordetella (kennel cough) vaccinations. You can bring a digital copy or paper certificate on your first appointment.

Most sessions take between 60 to 90 minutes depending on your pet's size, coat condition, and chosen service package. We never rush our groomers because gentle, stress-free handling takes precedence. We will text you 15 minutes before your pup is ready for pickup!

Dogs often get overly excited or anxious when seeing their owners nearby, which can make scissor work around sensitive areas less safe. You are very welcome to relax in our comfortable glass-partitioned reception lounge with free coffee and Wi-Fi while seeing your pup from afar.

Absolutely! Our stylists are certified in low-stress, fear-free handling. We use gentle distraction techniques, lick mats with peanut butter, soothing lavender aromatherapy, and noise-reducing Happy Hoodies to ensure nervous dogs feel safe and calm.

For curly or long coats (Doodles, Poodles, Shih Tzus), we recommend a professional grooming every 4 to 6 weeks to prevent painful matting. Short-haired breeds typically thrive on a bath and nail trim every 6 to 8 weeks. You can also use our interactive Estimator above for personalized recommendations!
